# Launch notes

## One-line description

Focusline is a free, private to-do tracker for today, this week and this month. Your activities stay in your browser.

## Short description (about 50 words)

Focusline helps you keep track of what needs doing today, this week and this month. Add activities with due dates and priorities, mark them done, and see overdue items at a glance. There are no accounts and no tracking. Your data stays in your browser, with export and import for backups. It works offline and can be installed on your phone or computer.
